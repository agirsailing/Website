Time until we take Garda by storm

Milestones timeline

Competing

June 2026


The week in Malcesine for the SuMoth Challenge was full of setbacks and learning experiences, with limited sailing time, one completed race despite technical issues, and a strong team effort that highlighted both our resilience and the valuable lessons we’ll carry forward into building a fully functional boat for next season.

Bringing everything together

June 2026


This month marked the exciting transition from building Gungnir in the workshop to sailing it on Lake Garda, with successful assembly, structural testing, valuable design improvements, our first races on the water, and the unforgettable debut of our bright pink boat proving that we’re truly #TooPinkToSink.

Finish the manufacturing of components

May 2026


This month has brought major progress across the SuMoth project, with key structural components and foils nearing completion, continued advances in sail manufacturing, innovative sustainable manufacturing techniques, and important preparations for Garda, including our first newsletter and ongoing S2 report, as the boat rapidly comes together.

Beginning of manufacturing

April 2026


This month has marked a major step from design to reality, with the successful construction and testing of our wings, continued progress on the hull, the arrival of essential materials from our sponsors, and the completion of our S1 report, bringing us ever closer to seeing our SuMoth take to the water in Garda this summer.

Design locked

March 2026


This month has been about turning our SuMoth vision into reality, with major progress on the hull and CAD design, valuable sponsor outreach at Allt för Sjön, stronger team collaboration both in and outside the workshop, and the completion of our first vlog as we continue moving closer to competing in Garda this summer.

First sponsors

February 2026


This month marked an exciting step forward for our SuMoth journey, with new sponsors and partners joining the project, key resources such as our hull and reference boat falling into place, and significant engineering progress through testing and structural analysis, bringing us one step closer to competing in Garda this summer.

Start of the concrete work on our moth

January 2026


We are at the very beginning of our journey as a brand new SuMoth team from KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm. We’re currently in the final stages of structuring the team, defining roles, and laying the foundation for the work ahead.

Registration to the SuMoth Challenge

December 2025


After deciding to participate in the 2026 edition, our team was officialy created with high ambitions and determination.
