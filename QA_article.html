A quick talk on sustainability

s

Agïr Sailing Team embraces the SuMoth Challenge core values, not only as a performance-driven competition but also as a sustainability-focused challenge.

As a new team joining the 2026 edition, the sustainability vision has been at the core of the project from the very beginning. Through adaptive design processes and the integration of upcycled materials, our moth, Gugnir, is intended to be one of the most sustainable moths possible.

My name is Charlotte, and together with Oscar and Giulia, I am in charge of the sustainability aspects within the Ägir Sailing Team. Since January, we have been evaluating the environmental performance of Gugnir using the competition’s virtual currency, SuMoth dollar (SM$). Materials as well as manufacturing processes were assigned a ‘cost’, and we ensured that the total stayed within the allowed budget of 10,000 SM$. In parallel, we conducted a Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) using the software MarineShift360. This marine industry LCA tool allowed us to assess the environmental impact of the boat across its entire lifespan: from material sourcing to end-of-life.

More than just evaluating, our goal is to give the team a clearer direction from which to pursue sustainability. The MarineShift360 dashboard allowed us to identify high-impact elements and where we should focus our efforts, either by adopting different materials or by other means of reducing the environmental footprint of the manufacturing.

With this, Ägir Sailing Team proves that design, performance, and sustainability can evolve together, paving the way for future generations of high-performance sailing boats.
